Project overview

Leave Management application is customize solution for any office having teams and teams leads. User can register an account, admin approves it. user apply for leave prior 11a.m or he cannot apply for leave for the same day. Short leaves can also be applied. User marks leave to Team Lead, who either sends it forward to CEO or rejects it. CEO can reject or approve or send leave further to Chairman. Once leave is approved user will get notification. Team can chat or share media with eachother. Stats are shown to admin in website and mobile application. with many options

security features like VPN check and cross country access check in place. User cannot apply for leave with VPN enabled or sitting out side country and apply
